How to play

Joining a game

Join the queue. When 5 players are in it, a 30 second countdown starts. When it reaches zero the game begins and every player gets a secret role.

The phases

A game moves through four phases. After Voting, it goes back to Intermission and repeats.

PhaseLengthWhat happens
Intermission15 secondsA short break before the next night.
Night60 secondsThe Mafia can shoot a player.
Discussion60 secondsTalk it through. The Vigilante can shoot a player.
Voting30 secondsVote for a player to eject, or skip.

Shooting

When someone is shot, the player is dead and the phase timer drops to 15 seconds.

Voting

Each living player gets one vote. Your vote is permanent until Voting ends, so choose carefully.

Factions

Every role belongs to one of three factions.

Mafia

Stay hidden and take out everyone else. Roles: Mafia, Witch.

Neutral

Has its own goal, separate from the other factions. Roles: Jester.

Citizens

Work out who the Mafia are. Roles: Bodyguard, Spy, Vigilante, Doctor, Detective, Ringmaster, Judge.

Jester Neutral faction

If the Mafia kill you during the night, you lose. If you get voted out, or the Vigilante shoots you, you win and can bring 1 person with you to the afterlife.
